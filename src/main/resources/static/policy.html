Procurement policy as data

Which route a request takes, who approves it and how many quotes it needs are rules in a versioned policy, not code. A route is a sequence of building blocks. Every request keeps the version it was submitted under. Not configurable: nobody approves their own request, budget figures never reach the assistant, nothing is ordered without a budget reservation, and a strategic route is discussed with the employee first.
